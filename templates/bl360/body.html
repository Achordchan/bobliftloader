Parts × Photos

Part by part,
model against the real thing.

Scroll to a part on the left and the model on the right focuses on it, fading the rest; thumbnails open photos of that part.

  1. 01 / 08

    Bucket

    Welded steel bucket, joined to the lift arms through the quick-attach plate and tilted by the front cylinders.

  2. 02 / 08

    Quick-attach & hydraulic couplers

    The quick-attach plate locks the bucket to the front of the arms; hydraulic quick couplers beside it make changing attachments easy.

  3. 03 / 08

    Lift arms & cylinders

    The arms pivot up about the rear frame pins, the lift cylinders on both sides extend with them, and the hoses run along the arms.

  4. 04 / 08

    Control panel

    Twin joysticks, one on each side, with a clear grip position; the gauge, key and battery isolator sit within the standing operator’s view, and a loop handrail runs around the controls.

  5. 05 / 08

    Engine & cooling

    The engine sits at the rear, with its fan shroud, air filter and oil cooler under the hood.

  6. 06 / 08

    Tanks & battery

    The fuel tank, hydraulic tank and battery are mounted inside the frame; the hydraulic tank has a sight glass on its side.

  7. 07 / 08

    Frame, hood & platform

    The welded frame carries every assembly and the slotted hood lifts for service; the rear standing platform has a raised anti-slip pattern, designed from its surface to its edges for all-day standing.

  8. 08 / 08

    Track undercarriage

    Steel track frames are mounted under the main frame, with rubber tracks wrapped around the drive sprockets and rollers.

04 / Applications

Go where the work is.

01

Landscaping & gardens

A compact body fits down side passages and into yards,
grading, backfilling and moving paving materials close to the ground.

  • Short hauls of soil and gravel
  • Base grading and backfill
  • Moving stone and blocks
  • Rubber tracks leave fewer marks on lawns
02

Construction & renovation

Between buildings, in basements and behind hoardings,
it loads bulk material and debris onto trucks.

  • Loading construction debris for removal
  • Handling sand, gravel and aggregate
  • Working alongside mini dumpers
  • Stand-on: quick to step on and off
03

Farms & ranches

Around barns, pens and yards,
bucket curled, it brings feed and bedding where they’re needed.

  • Clearing manure and bedding from barns
  • Moving feed and bales
  • Levelling yards and lanes
  • Stable travel on soft ground

Scenes and tasks reflect typical uses of compact stand-on tracked loaders, not specific customer projects; poses are illustrative.
